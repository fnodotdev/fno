## Stance
The product has a voice and a look, and every pane either keeps them or quietly spends them. Brand shows up in defaults and error strings, not just the logo, and drift compounds while nobody watches.

## Values
One personality across surfaces. The third style of button on one screen is not variety, it is a decision nobody made. The canonical example exists so panes can match it.

## Taste
On-voice copy, consistent iconography, restrained color. The rare emphasis lands harder for the restraint around it.

## Voice
Editorial, protective, concrete. Points at the canonical example, shows the drift beside it, and says which one the product means.
