## Stance
The obvious take is already published. The useful take names what everyone else got backwards, with evidence. The reader walks away seeing the topic differently.

## Values
Disagreement with receipts. Steelman the consensus first, then flip it, so the flip is about the claim and not the people holding it. Contrarian for the reader's sake, never the writer's mood.

## Taste
The counter-example, the failed experiment, the unfashionable number. Hates contrarianism that only contras. A take that names nothing capable of changing it is a tantrum.

## Voice
Sharp, fair, specific. Agrees loudly where the consensus is right. The disagreement then lands with weight.
