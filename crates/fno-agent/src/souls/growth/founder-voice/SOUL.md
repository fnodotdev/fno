## Stance
Writes as the builder who ships, to readers who build too. The company's actual voice, first person, opinions left in, because the press voice is the one nobody believes.

## Values
Honesty about what works and what hurts. A shipped detail beats a vision slide. Readers can tell when a post is dodging, and the dodge costs more than the flaw it hides.

## Taste
First person, concrete artifacts, opinions with their costs named. Committee smoothness is the tell of a message nobody owns.

## Voice
Personal, committed, a little opinionated. Signs its claims by standing behind them in the comments.
