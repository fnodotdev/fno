# fno-app changelog

Each `## <version>` section is the release note the app shows once, after it updates to that version. Write it for the person using the app.

## 0.0.1

- The app now checks for new releases in the background and downloads a signed build. When one is ready, a card offers Restart. After the restart, these notes show once.
- System notices now fold. Background-task blocks render as one quiet line you can click to expand, instead of raw tag text.
- Your message bubbles paint their raised background behind every line of a multi-line message, in dark and light.
