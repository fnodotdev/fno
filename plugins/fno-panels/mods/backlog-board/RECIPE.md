# Backlog board

Open it to see what work is queued, in progress or done.

Each piece of work is a card. The columns run from intake to done, with the card detail beside them.

## What it runs

Nothing of its own. It is a native mod: the mux paints this view itself, and the entry here makes it list beside every other mod. Open it from the mux menu or with `fno backlog board`.

Native views ship with fno, so they cannot be switched off.
