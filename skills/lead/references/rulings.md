# Recording a ruling, in full

One hop from [Recording a ruling](../SKILL.md#recording-a-ruling). The root keeps the rules; this page keeps every flag, refusal and channel detail.

A titled lead answers the open questions in its scope, and escalates only what the superuser must decide. The rules:

- **Rule the question that sits with you.** A worker's ask goes to its lead first. This is `questions.route=ladder`, the default. The lead gets one wake. Rule what your rules allow, and give the why: `fno inbox outstanding clear <qid> --answer "<ruling>" --authority role`. If you lack the context, spawn a fresh-context subagent for evidence first. If only the user can rule it, climb it: `fno inbox outstanding climb <qid> --evidence "<what you checked>"`. "Not known yet, here is what we checked" is a valid climb. A confident guess is a defect. An unanswered row reaches the user's board 10 minutes after it reached you.
- **File your own user-only decision.** Only a lead files to the user's board. It tries the answer first. Then it runs `fno inbox outstanding ask` with the `## Tried` and `## Evidence` sections. `fno agents hq escalate` records its question with no relay mail. `direct` and `triage` opt out of the ladder. The filing session carries the mobile handoff. A successful ask (or a climb that lands on the user's board) prints a `claude-push:` line. The ask-push hook turns that line into exactly one PushNotification from this session. The message holds the question number, the decision, and the recommended option, under 200 chars. The tap opens this session. The user answers in chat. Record the answer with `fno inbox outstanding clear <qid> --answer "<the user's words>" --authority user`. Workers never push. A worker's ask wakes your rung, and your session pushes. One push per question. Routine progress never pushes. ntfy stays the second path.
- **Answer.** Your role clears two kinds of question: one it asked, and one the ladder placed on it. Run `fno inbox outstanding clear <qid> --answer "<answer, with one line of why>" --authority role`. A class in `questions.rules.deny` refuses the clear and names the climb. Any other question on the board is the user's, and the clear refuses a role on it. The answer records as coordination and closes the question.
- **Withdraw a moot question.** A worker in your scope asked, and then the worker stopped or its node finished. Close it with `fno inbox outstanding clear <qid> --answer "<the ruling that made it moot>" --authority role`. The clear records a withdrawal, never a user answer. It refuses while the asker lives on an open node, and it refuses a node outside your scope.
- **Escalate the deny classes only.** The default `questions.rules.deny` holds five classes. They are `irreversible`, `money-security`, `outside-reach`, `taste` and `law-change`. A lead rules the `ask` classes itself: `public-surface` and `gate-override`. Everything else, decide and log. The escalation is one note in the escalations directory, at `fno-agents state path escalations`. It has five sections. They are what is being decided, why it matters now, the options, the recommendation, and what happens on silence. State a deadline. No bare ids.
- **Silence has a default.** Past the deadline the check-in names the default. Take the recommended option and record it with `fno inbox decide`. When the call is irreversible, wait instead. A question with no deadline gets one a day after it was asked, and its default is to wait. A `wait` question surfaces once, then once more for each further day.
- **A held question is not news.** Report a held question that is new, overdue, or asked about by the user. Leave every other held question out. Repeating one each beat is nagging. The user is away or busy, and the question waits on their list. When the user says later, snooze it with their words: `fno inbox outstanding snooze <qid> --until <YYYY-MM-DD|reply> --quote "<their words>"`. Use `reply` for a `wait` question. A take-recommended question takes a date, and its default fires at the snooze end. Between beats, decide what D3290 lets you decide and move other work.
- **When the superuser answers in chat, record it.** `fno inbox law set` for a law change, else `fno inbox decide <node> "<answer>" --authority role --rationale "superuser in chat: <their words>"`, and set the note's `status`. A harness with a push notification tool also sends one that names the note.
- **The fleet reads Visual Log taps.** The user approves a look with a tap on the Visual Log page. A dedicated fleet session runs the reader (skills/visual-log-reader/). Never arm a per-session reader cron, because two readers double-record approvals and a lead's cron dies with the lead. When the probe in [references/visual-log-taps.md](visual-log-taps.md) exits 1, run the named repair.

A titled lead is not the superuser: the `operator` authority is refused on an agent session, and law stays superuser tier. The lead's channels:


- `fno backlog note <node> <text>` for a finding or a ruling against a row. It mails the row's live holder and the epic's lead, so a ruling reaches the worker without a second call. When the row names no live holder to tell, it exits 3 and writes nothing. Read the refusal, then mail a reader by name or pass `--quiet`. `--quiet` writes the note and mails nobody. A ruling that conditions a merge needs a merge hold: [Merge holds](cli-commands.md#merge-holds).
- `fno inbox law set <subject> <decision> --rationale "<why>" --quote "<the user's words>"` for a durable rule the user asked for. It records a chat-attested row and can never supersede the superuser's own law.
- `fno agents hq faq add --question "..." --answer "..." --specimen "<node or PR>, <date>" --exit "<the change that retires this>"` for a durable answer a successor lead will ask for. It refuses without `--exit`, the change that stops the answer being needed. The three channels divide this way: a FAQ entry answers a question a successor will ask, a note records a finding against one row, and a law records an operator ruling.

Read a ruling back with `fno backlog decisions <subject>` or `fno inbox decisions <subject> --lane law`, newest first. A subject matches exactly, so never mint a near-synonym. Every ruling is machine-local project policy. A rule that a stranger cloning the repository must obey does not reach them from here. Land it in the code, a doc or a gate, in a PR. See [decision-record](../../../docs/architecture/decision-record.md).
